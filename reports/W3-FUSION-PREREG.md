## Labels are re-frozen after Patrick's pass

`reports/W3-LABEL-SHEET.csv` lists the 24 flagged chunks for Patrick's verdicts. The sheet is blind. **The labeller sees exactly these columns and nothing else:** row id, question id (opaque, `Q01`..), question text, opaque chunk id (`C01`..), chunk text, a blank verdict column and a blank notes column. No prior grades, labelled passages, hit status, strength remarks, retrieval ranks, ranker names, group or split names appear, and rows are shuffled with a fixed seed so order carries no signal. None of this fusion test needs the labeller to see ranks or rankers. The real `wesley-NNN-cNNNN` chunk ids reveal the sermon number, so they are not on the sheet. The mapping from row, question and chunk ids back to real ids, plus all the withheld context, is in `reports/W3-LABEL-SHEET-KEY.md`, withheld from the labeller until verdicts are recorded. The sheet and KEY are generated by `scripts/make_label_sheet.py` (seed recorded in the KEY) and `tests/test_label_sheet.py` checks the sheet reproduces byte-for-byte and carries no leaking tags. **Labels are to be re-frozen after that pass and before the run**: apply the accepted verdicts to `eval/questions.jsonl` in their own commit, record its hash here, and run nothing until then. No label may change between that commit and the final report. Labels are never edited after seeing a run.

## Frozen change

- Only `Searcher.hybrid` with `fusion="rrf"` changes. It passes `weights=[1.0, w]` to `reciprocal_rank_fusion` (semantic list first, BM25 list second), so `w` is the BM25 weight.
- **Grid: w ∈ {0.5, 0.25}.** No other value is tried. The baseline is w = 1.0 (shipped).
- **OR-shape rule, exact:** `w` applies only when `to_fts_query(query)` (`src/preached/fts.py`) returns a string built with `" OR "`. That is: `scripture_match(query)` is `None` and `content_terms(query)` has more than 4 terms. The AND form (1 to 4 content terms, or no content terms) and the NEAR form (scripture reference) keep weights `[1, 1]`. The rule is evaluated on the query string only, never on results or group labels. The `weighted` fusion is untouched. RRF k stays 60.

## Selection

- **Selection groups:** `plain` (25), `names-topic` (23), `title-keyword` (3), `scripture-keyword` (7). Unanswerable items are not scored.
- Metric: lenient hit@10 on the raw ranker path (`search/bench.py` without `--dedupe`), `granite-english-r2`, with the re-frozen labels. Pick the `w` with the higher pooled hit@10 over the selection groups. **A tie goes to the milder weight (0.5).**
- Both weights are also reported in full for every group, with the paired win/tie/loss counts against baseline.
- **`plain-blind` is reported once, after `w` is chosen, as an in-sample check only.** It is not held out and not confirmatory. It does not influence the choice, and it is not rerun after any other change.

## Required reruns

- `search/bench.py --abstain` on baseline and the chosen `w`, on both the raw and `--dedupe` paths. Report the abstain rate on answerable and on unanswerable items.
- `--dedupe` on baseline and chosen `w` for all groups.
- The abstain floor in `search/abstain.toml` is **not refit**, and not on proposed labels.

## Fresh-blind-set confirmation (before any accuracy claim)

Write 20 to 30 new questions, by a person or by an LLM that has not seen the corpus or this repo's questions. The writer must not be a model from a PRC-based developer (AGENTS.md §3), and must not have seen `reports/W3-RETRIEVAL-DIAG.md` or this prereg. The set is confirmatory and is required before any accuracy claim, not only before shipping. Freeze the wording and the labels and commit them before any ranker runs on the set; no ranker may have been run on them. Labelling is done by someone who has not seen the baseline or weighted rankings for them. Relevant passages are found before any ranker runs, by reading the sermons and by plain text search (grep) of the corpus, as in W1-B. FTS5/BM25 and dense search are rankers and may not be used to find them. Then run baseline and the chosen `w` once.

Each question carries a provenance field (for example `person-written` or `llm-written:<model>`). If an LLM writes the set, the report discloses it as LLM-written, and says whether the labels were confirmed by a person.

## What means "do not ship"

Any of the following:

1. The chosen `w` has a negative paired hit@10 difference on the pooled selection groups.
2. It loses more than one question in any one selection group, or any in `title-keyword` or `scripture-keyword` (those must be unchanged by construction; a change means the rule is wrong).
3. On the fresh blind set, the 95% paired-bootstrap CI (10,000 resamples, seed recorded) of the paired hit@10 difference includes zero and at least one fresh-set question is lost. The CI is the percentile interval from `preached.stats.paired_bootstrap` (default seed 20261030). A question is lost when a relevant passage is in the top 10 under the baseline and none is in the top 10 under the chosen `w`.
4. On the fresh blind set, the chosen `w` does not beat baseline in hit@10.
5. The abstain behaviour or `--dedupe` results degrade versus baseline (more false answers on unanswerable items, or lower hit@10 with `--dedupe`).

`plain-blind` is in-sample and gates nothing; a loss there is reported as a warning.

A mixed result is reported as mixed. It is not rescued by adding a third weight, changing the rule, or editing labels.
